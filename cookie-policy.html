What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser to remember certain preferences, analyze traffic, and support advertising systems.

How We Use Cookies

Cookies help us:

  • Display ads effectively
  • Measure audience engagement
  • Limit repeated ads
  • Improve website performance
  • Store cookie consent acknowledgment

Types of Cookies Used

  • Essential: Required for the site to function
  • Analytics: Helps us understand visitor behaviour
  • Advertising: Enables personalized or non-personalized ads via third-party vendors

Managing or Disabling Cookies

You can block or delete cookies anytime from your browser settings. If you reject cookies, some features may be limited and ads will not be personalized.

Third-Party Cookies

Ad providers such as Google may store their own cookies when ads load. These cookies are governed by their respective platforms, not IDMG directly.
