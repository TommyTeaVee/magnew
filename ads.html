Advertising & Sponsorship Policy

IMPILO Magazine is a digital media brand under Impilo Digital Media Group. We are transparent about how advertising, sponsorship, and brand collaborations work across our platforms.

We offer digital advertising services including banner placements, video commercials, sponsored articles, runway promotion packages, catalogue listings, and streaming ad slots. All advertising is reviewed internally to meet our editorial and audience standards.

Sponsored content, paid placements, or collaborations are clearly labelled as “Sponsored”, “Ad”, or “Brand Partner Content”. We never intentionally disguise paid content as purely editorial coverage.

We do not accept advertising that promotes illegal drugs, weapons, explicit adult content, discrimination, hate speech, or misleading medical, financial, or political claims. Our focus is on safe, creative, youth-relevant content such as fashion, lifestyle, culture, entrepreneurship, and technology.

Third-party ad and analytics partners (for example, Google Ads or Meta ads platforms) may be used to deliver and measure campaigns. These services may use cookies or aggregated data to improve relevance and performance. We do not sell sensitive personal information.

Model and talent promotion takes place with the permission of the individuals represented by Impilo Modelling Agency. Any collaborations or bookings involving our talent follow ethical standards and clear communication.

Our revenue from ads, sponsorships, and media projects helps us sustain and grow IMPILO Magazine while maintaining a focus on honest, non-exploitative storytelling and representation.

For collaboration, media kit requests, or rate card enquiries, please contact us via our official social profiles: @impilomag on Instagram, Facebook, X, and YouTube.
