Editorial & Commercial Modeling

Our editorial and commercial division represents models for campaigns, magazines, digital ads and branded content. Talent is trained to embody story, style and brand identity with confidence and professionalism.

  • Full editorial photoshoots for magazines, lookbooks and digital campaigns.
  • Commercial modeling for advertisements, OOH and online platforms.
  • On-set coaching for posing, expression and movement.
  • Portfolio-ready images aligned to industry standards.

To apply, send a simple headshot, full-length photo and short introduction to agency@impilomag.co.za.

Fashion Runway

Professional runway training and casting for fashion shows and live events, including the Exclusive Jozi Fashion Runway. We focus on walk, presence, styling and stage discipline.

  • Runway coaching: walk, turns, posture and timing with music.
  • Participation in local fashion shows and Jozi Fashion Runway.
  • Guidance on styling, fittings and backstage professionalism.
  • Runway photos and clips for your portfolio and social media.

Shortlisted models may be invited to walk at the Exclusive Jozi Fashion Runway in partnership with designers and IMPILO.

Professional Modeling Photoshoot

High-quality studio and location shoots designed to build or update your portfolio. We focus on clean, agency-ready images that reflect your range and personality.

  • Studio and outdoor sessions with professional lighting.
  • Beauty, fashion, lifestyle and character looks in one package.
  • Images edited for comp cards, social media and casting profiles.
  • Optional hair, makeup and styling add-ons.

Sessions are available for new faces and working models; rates are shared once your application is reviewed.

Social Media & Brand Influence

We help models and creatives build authentic, professional social media presence, aligned with brand partnerships and long-term career goals.

  • Profile review and content strategy for Instagram, TikTok and more.
  • Guidance on brand collaborations and sponsored content.
  • Content planning for shoots, reels and behind-the-scenes.
  • Connection with IMPILO Streaming and podcast platforms.

We focus on long-term brand building, not just quick trends – especially for models appearing in IMPILO and our digital shows.

Performance Arts (Voice, Drama & Film)

Some models are also performers. We provide a path for those interested in acting, voice work and on-camera performance for film, commercial and stage.

  • On-camera training for commercials and digital content.
  • Intro to acting, script reading and character work.
  • Voice-over basics for narration and advertising.
  • Showreel guidance and integration with portfolio.

Ideal for multi-talented creatives who want to combine modeling with drama, voice and film opportunities.

Portfolio Development & Casting Reels

We design complete portfolios: from images and comp cards to video reels that show your walk, movement and presence on camera.

  • Concept planning for portfolio (fashion, lifestyle, beauty and more).
  • Casting reels to show walk, turns and personality.
  • Digital comp cards for agencies, clients and castings.
  • Guidance on updating your portfolio annually.

We aim for long-term growth – building materials that help you step into local and international opportunities.
